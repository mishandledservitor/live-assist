Captions that were on the page before Start are not kept. `Download transcript` saves one file, from the main view, a Transcripts row or a meeting's transcript alike: `Meeting — DD-MM-YYYY at HHhMM.zip` holding `… — Transcript.txt` and `… — Meet captions.txt` when the meeting has both, the plain `… — Transcript.txt` when it has only a transcript (the name every downloaded transcript now carries), and the plain `… — Meet captions.txt` when captions were kept and nothing was transcribed. The captions file has the transcript's line shape and names (your name for Meet's "You", renames applied). During a meeting it has the captions so far. The zip is written by the extension itself (stored, UTF-8 names), with no new permission and no new dependency. Three texts say what is kept and deleted: on a Meet call the reminder before Start adds "Meet's captions of the meeting are kept on this computer too.", and the delete confirmations on the main view and the Transcripts screen name Meet's captions for a meeting that has them; everything else reads as before. Fixed while building: a turn Meet keeps writing into for more than an hour (one person talking alone) lost everything after the hour, because the Meet bridge dropped text for a turn first seen that long ago; and a new caption turn opening with the same word as an earlier one could, after captions were turned off and on, replace the earlier turn's text. The real-call check awaits the user (`specs/013-captions-download/manual-check.md`); what Meet's page does on a reload, in a call over an hour, in a five-minute monologue and when captions are turned off and on is recorded there.
